About the workshop

Making forgetting a first-class capability of AI

Machine unlearning seeks to remove the influence of designated data, concepts, identities or knowledge from trained models while preserving their broader utility

The need spans privacy and data-removal requirements, copyright concerns, model correction, safety, bias mitigation and responsible AI deployment

MULVA brings together researchers and practitioners working across computer vision, natural language processing, multimodal learning, generative AI and foundation models to discuss methods, evaluation, verification and real-world challenges
