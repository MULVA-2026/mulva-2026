Call for Presentations

Topics of Interest

Research advancing the foundations, methods, evaluation and deployment of machine unlearning

Submission Information

Submission deadlines, formats, publication arrangements and submission links will be announced in accordance with ICVGIP 2026 policies. Please check this page for updates

01Foundations, formulations and theoretical guarantees of machine unlearning
02Exact, approximate, certified and parameter-efficient unlearning methods
03Data-, instance-, class-, concept-, identity- and knowledge-level unlearning
04Unlearning in computer vision, NLP, LLMs and visual foundation models
05Unlearning in multimodal, vision-language, generative and foundation models
06Model editing, knowledge removal and their relationship to machine unlearning
07Efficient, scalable, federated, distributed and continual unlearning
08Privacy, copyright, data deletion, safety, fairness and responsible AI
09Adversarial attacks, membership inference, privacy leakage and verification of forgetting
10Evaluation metrics, benchmarks, datasets and forgetting–retention trade-offs
11Unlearning for multimodal agents and real-world AI systems
12Industrial deployment, lifecycle management and open challenges in trustworthy computational unlearning

Possible Contribution Types

  • Novel unlearning algorithms and theoretical analyses
  • Empirical and systematic evaluations
  • Computer vision, NLP and multimodal unlearning studies
  • Datasets, benchmarks and evaluation protocols
  • Privacy and security analyses
  • Position and perspective papers on open challenges
