Submission Guidelines

Preparing Your Submission

Please review the following guidance before submitting your manuscript to the MULVA 2026 workshop

Paper Format and Length

  • Manuscripts should be submitted in PDF format
  • The main manuscript should not exceed 8 pages, including text, figures and tables
  • References may extend beyond the 8-page limit
  • The maximum PDF file size is 20 MB
  • Authors are encouraged to use the official ICVGIP 2026 paper template

Double-Blind Review

  • Submissions must follow the double-blind review policy
  • Author names and affiliations must not appear in the manuscript
  • Acknowledgements and other identifying information should be omitted
  • References to prior work should not reveal the authors' identities

Originality and Submission Policy

  • Submitted manuscripts should represent original research contributions
  • Submissions must comply with the applicable ICVGIP originality and concurrent-submission policies
  • Authors should ensure that their manuscripts do not violate publication or copyright policies
  • Please consult the official conference guidelines for the complete policy

Supplementary Material

  • Supplementary material may be provided where it supports the submitted work
  • Examples include additional experiments, proofs, results, code and datasets
  • Supplementary material should not contain identifying information during double-blind review
  • Authors should follow the file and upload requirements specified by OpenReview

For the complete author instructions and official paper template, please visit the ICVGIP 2026 website. Please check the MULVA OpenReview page for workshop-specific submission requirements

Explore the Research Landscape

Topics of Interest

We welcome research spanning the theory, algorithms, evaluation and practical deployment of machine unlearning across language, vision and multimodal AI. Topics include, but are not limited to, the following

01

Foundations and Theoretical Guarantees

Foundations, formulations and theoretical guarantees of machine unlearning

02

Unlearning Methods and Algorithms

Exact, approximate, certified and parameter-efficient unlearning methods

03

Granularity of Forgetting

Data-, instance-, class-, concept-, identity- and knowledge-level unlearning

04

Vision and Language Models

Unlearning in computer vision, NLP, LLMs and visual foundation models

05

Multimodal and Generative AI

Unlearning in multimodal, vision-language, generative and foundation models

06

Model Editing and Knowledge Removal

Model editing, knowledge removal and their relationship to machine unlearning

07

Scalable and Continual Unlearning

Efficient, scalable, federated, distributed and continual unlearning

08

Privacy and Responsible AI

Privacy, copyright, data deletion, safety, fairness and responsible AI

09

Security and Verification

Adversarial attacks, membership inference, privacy leakage and verification of forgetting

10

Evaluation and Benchmarking

Evaluation metrics, benchmarks, datasets and forgetting–retention trade-offs

11

Multimodal Agents and AI Systems

Unlearning for multimodal agents and real-world AI systems

12

Deployment and Open Challenges

Industrial deployment, lifecycle management and open challenges in trustworthy computational unlearning
